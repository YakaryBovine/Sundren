You've known about the Sunlancer since you were young; it's a violently bright object that hangs proudly in the sky. Occasionally, it fires an immense beam of solar energy underneath itself, granting temporary access to one of the Stålråd's supply depots, Forsyning.

At the beginning of your adventure, you delved into Forsyning's Supply Depot D and discovered many unusual riches. You also noted an interest from two other groups; a group of mages you now know to be from Splint, and a trio of Earth Elementals named Koom'durg.

Within Forsyning, you found that some of the Warforged inhabitants once feared an entity named Furls-His-Wings, who they believed was responsible for leveling the Sunlancer against them.

After witnessing the Sunlancer's blast for the second time, an angel from the sky and landed at your feet.
