Everyone knows that the Last War was started because the Stålråd could not tolerate the Urzithan's use of magic, and the Urzithans could not tolerate a society that rejected their Gods. But you want to know more - concretely, what are the historical events that precipitated the near-total destruction of the world?

Soren asked you to locate a near-space observatory within Draumheim, and use it to identify the nature of a number of stars that appear to move strangely through the night sky. You found it and, after confronting its sole inhabitant and his defenses, used the observatory scope. Through it, you saw that the stars were in fact high-orbit satellites that had once been equipped with an array of nuclear missiles - now clearly depleted. The computer systems revealed that these missiles were launched at the planet as part of Operation Oblivion, a last-ditch and successful Stålråd effort to deprive the Urzithan Triumvirate of their powers.

You discovered a maddened simulacrum of Skofnildr (see [[The Fourth Sect]]), who revealed to you that he was the reason the Triumvirate could not unleash their full powers on the Stålråd. It was his defeat that allowed the Triumvirate to reenter the war, and their reentrance that forced the Stålråd to initiate Project Oblivion.
